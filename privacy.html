Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Обновлено: 10.10.2026

aidar.fin publisher is an internal tool of TOO «Aikar-KZ» (Center for Financial Business Solutions, Almaty, Kazakhstan, aidarfin.kz). It is used only by our own team to publish our own educational videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and LinkedIn. It is not offered to third-party users.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos and set their titles and descriptions on our own YouTube channel. By using this application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What information we access, collect and store

When the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the application receives an access token and a refresh token with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly, and reads basic channel information (channel title and ID) to confirm the correct channel. We do not access, collect or store data of any other YouTube users or viewers, and we do not download YouTube content.

How we use and share it

Tokens are used only to upload our own scheduled videos and to read the status of those uploads. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties. Information is not used for advertising.

Storage, cookies and devices

Tokens are stored in an encrypted secrets store (GitHub Actions secrets) and on the owner's workstation. The application does not place cookies or other tracking technologies on users' devices.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. On revocation or on request (aisuleimenov@gmail.com) we delete all stored tokens and channel data within 7 days. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.

Other platforms

For Instagram, Threads, TikTok and LinkedIn the application likewise stores only the access tokens of our own accounts and uses them only to publish our own content.
